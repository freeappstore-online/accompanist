Caro mio ben (Come once again)
Attributed to Giuseppe Giordani (1751-1798); some scholars, among them John Glenn Paton,
credit Tommaso Giordani (c. 1730-1806).

Edition: Mutopia Project, typeset in LilyPond by Peter Chubb (2009) from the
Boosey and Hawkes 1903 edition, as corrected from other editions.
https://www.mutopiaproject.org/ftp/GiordanoG/caromioben/

The typesetter placed this sheet music in the public domain:
http://creativecommons.org/licenses/publicdomain

Accompanist's score.musicxml and accompaniment.mid are derived from the edition's
LilyPond source and MIDI by pipeline/caro_mio_ben.py: the voice and lyric tracks are
removed from the MIDI so only the piano plays, and grace notes are left out of the
on-screen score. These derived files are also dedicated to the public domain.
