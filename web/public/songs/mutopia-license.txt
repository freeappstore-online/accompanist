Art songs in Accompanist come from the Mutopia Project (https://www.mutopiaproject.org),
whose volunteers typeset public-domain editions in LilyPond. Each song's source file is
listed in web/songs-provenance.json in the app's repository, with its typesetter and the
printed edition it was taken from shown in the app's About page.

Only editions the typesetter placed in the public domain are used:
http://creativecommons.org/licenses/publicdomain

Accompanist's score.musicxml and accompaniment.mid for each song are derived from the
LilyPond source by pipeline/build.py: LilyPond reads the source, the voice part is left out
of the playback so only the piano plays, and grace notes are left out of score and playback.
These derived files are also dedicated to the public domain.

Caro mio ben is attributed to Giuseppe Giordani (1751-1798); some scholars, among them
John Glenn Paton, credit Tommaso Giordani (c. 1730-1806).
